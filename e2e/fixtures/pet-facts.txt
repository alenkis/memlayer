Fun Facts About Household Pets

Cats sleep for an average of 12 to 16 hours per day. Domestic cats are obligate carnivores, meaning they require nutrients found only in meat. A group of cats is called a clowder.

Dogs can understand up to 250 words and gestures. The Basenji is the only breed of dog that cannot bark. A dog's sense of smell is roughly 40 times more sensitive than a human's.

Goldfish have a memory span of at least three months, contrary to the popular myth of a three-second memory. They can recognize their owners and be trained to perform simple tricks.
